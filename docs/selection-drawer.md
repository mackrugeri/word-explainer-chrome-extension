# Selection drawer and page-tools launcher

Selection tools open in a narrow right-edge drawer. The header’s Minimize control or Escape collapses it into a small edge tab. Click the tab to restore the same selection and results; restoration never starts a new AI request. Running answers finish and remain available while minimized. Clicking the page minimizes the drawer; scrolling and resizing leave it usable. Close dismisses the drawer and tab and stops its own speech.

The page-tools W launcher remains partly tucked above the viewport and faint while idle. Hover or keyboard focus reveals the complete button; only a click opens the tools below it. Touch devices retain a larger visible target, and reduced-motion preferences disable the reveal transition. These changes use CSS and the existing dock timer; no new polling or observers were added.

Verified with the extension suite and real Chrome fixtures covering right-edge position, minimize/restore with an in-flight result, no duplicate request, keyboard focus/Escape, small screens, W hover/focus/idle, Review, references, selection activation and Google Skills frame tools.

Settings → Explanations → Ask to LLM offers **Ask with ChatGPT**, **Ask with Gemini**, or **Ask me first** (the initial default). A saved provider starts directly when Ask to LLM is clicked, including from page-tools selection shortcuts and PDF actions. Ask me first retains the existing provider chooser. Changes apply to open selection/PDF panels through the existing storage change event. This preference applies to Ask to LLM; other dedicated actions retain their provider behavior.

Selection tools share one compact button group. **Greek**, **Equation**, **References**, **Context** and **Notes** show their full action names in visible hover/focus tooltips and accessible labels. Tooltips render within the extension, stay within the viewport and dismiss on Escape, pointer exit, scrolling or minimizing. They do not rely on Chrome’s delayed title bubble. Button DOM order follows the displayed rows. The highlight palette stays below the scrollable tools/results region, above the footer.

The drawer has **Previous selection / Next selection** arrows and a position counter. It keeps up to 20 selections from the current page and current page session, with a 2 MB history budget. Each entry retains its answer sections and actual passage range. Browsing history restores the selection, scrolls its passage into view when needed, and shows saved answers without requesting another answer or duplicating Review entries. If the DOM changed, exact quote/context recovery is attempted; a missing passage is disclosed instead of selecting an unrelated word. Forward history branches when a new selection is opened after going back. Reloading or changing the page starts fresh history; persistent Review remains separate. Navigation remains available while an answer is running, and late results update their own history entry.
