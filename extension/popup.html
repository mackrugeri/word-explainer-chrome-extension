Word Explainer

Highlight a word or phrase on a web page. Press Command+Shift+Y (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+Y (Windows/Linux), then choose Pronounce or Explain in ChatGPT.

Customize the keyboard shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. If actions do not appear after an update, refresh the web page.

Sign in to ChatGPT in your browser to use your account. Protected Chrome pages are unsupported.
