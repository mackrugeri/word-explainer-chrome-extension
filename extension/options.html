Word Explainer settings

Select text, then press Command+Shift+Y on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+Y on Windows/Linux to show actions. Customize the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Translation uses Google Translate directly. Explanations use ChatGPT in an inactive tab and return to the selection panel. Sign in to ChatGPT beforehand if required. Website changes or verification may interrupt automation. Voice availability depends on your device.
